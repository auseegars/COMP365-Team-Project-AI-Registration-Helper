## Error interpretation

- **Ambiguous wording:** No specific intent keywords; review is needed.
- **Multiple categories:** The baseline refers multiple categories for review rather than breaking ties.
- **Unrecognized question:** Out-of-scope questions have no category rule.
- **Urgent without category:** Urgency is detected independently from routing.
- **Incidental date:** A time mention alone should not establish a deadline.
- **Simple negation:** Supported simple negation suppresses the explicit cue.
- **Empty input:** The classifier accepts blank strings for review; the submission form rejects blanks.
- **Punctuation and capitalization:** Normalization collapses punctuation and case.
- **Resolved hold context:** The broad hold rule ignores resolved context and can over-triage.
- **Complex urgency negation:** Negation outside the simple patterns can cause a false High suggestion.
- **Absolute deadline:** Absolute calendar dates are not interpreted; a real imminent deadline can be missed.
- **Unseen synonym:** Missing synonyms can hide a registration problem and its urgency.
- **Negation plus real deadline:** A supported near deadline remains High despite negated explicit urgency.
