# Recording checklist

- Rehearse the [video script](video_script.md) and time it with the actual app. Aim for 75–90 seconds within the team's single 10-minute video.
- Start Streamlit from the repo root using the [evidence setup](README.md). Verify **Overview** loads and that **Retrieved sources** appears for the demonstration question.
- Rehearse **SYN-CS-02** with **Cybersecurity**. Optional model use can change the elective ordering. Read the actual displayed ranking.
- If the interest dropdown is still open after choosing an option, press **Esc** before clicking **Generate recommendations**.
- Prefer a warmed local browser session and a readable window. Close unrelated tabs, silence notifications, use headphones or a quiet room, and turn your camera on.
- Record your own voice and keep your face visible. Show fictional data only. Keep the source link and prototype notice readable.
- Show **Retrieval only** if that is the mode displayed. When demonstrating generation, verify displayed claims against the passages first.
- Record the segment, review sound and text legibility, and send it to the teammate assembling the team video. Confirm all team members appear and verify submission instructions and upload receipt.
