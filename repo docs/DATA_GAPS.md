# Known gaps in the curriculum/transfer data (read before relying on it)

Honest list of what is *not* yet reliable. Updated 2026-10-25.

| # | Gap | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Prerequisites: 23 of the 201 non-COMP courses still unknown, none of them required courses** (`data/prereq_todo.csv`): ECEN 327 and ECEN 427 (EE elective-list courses not in the 2025-2026 catalog) and 21 gen-ed list courses (BIOL, BUAN, CST, FCS, FIN, JOMC, LIBS, MGMT, MKTG, MUSI, POLI, SLMG, SOCI, SSFM). The other 178 come from the 2025-2026 catalog course descriptions (ECON 200/201 from 2024-2025) in `data/catalog_prereqs.csv`. | The engine does not block on unknown ones; it lists them in `unverified_prereqs`. CIEN 483 "at least 3 of 5" is encoded exactly as 10 OR-groups. Placement-score alternatives (MATH 110, MATH 131, CHEM 106) are stored with no course requirement plus a `prereq_note`, so a student who placed in is not blocked. 8 courses that appear only as prerequisites (MATH 104, MATH 111, MATH 240, STAT 214, CST 101, CIEN 325, MEEN 315, MEEN 318) are `stub: true` entries. | Save the gen-ed prefix Course Descriptions pages and run `tools/parse_catalog_courses.py` then `tools/apply_prereqs.py`. Re-check each catalog year. |
| 2 | **Transfer map: 627 college rows from the A&T Transfer Articulation Tool (retrieved 2026-11-10) + a 29-row 2016 handbook seed (unverified, fallback only).** Rows are limited to courses in our 6 curricula; the tool is an unofficial guide (grade of C or higher required) and is updated daily. The 2016 seed disagrees with the tool in places (e.g. MAT 167 maps to MATH 123, not COMP 180, at all 10 colleges). Alamance came from copied text, not a saved page: grouping was rebuilt by page and spot-checked. | Courses the tool does not list for a college are reported as unmatched, not as "no credit". Non-curriculum listings are not yet counted as free-elective credit. A few A&T credit values differ from courses.json (MATH 110: 4 vs 3; ECEN 206: 3 vs 1) - see docs/TRANSFER_NOTES.md. | Re-save the pages each term and re-run tools/build_transfer.py; cross-check the UNC Transfer Course Guide; confirm with the Office of Transfer Articulation. |
| 3 | Catalog (Civil): course list sums to 121 credits, but the catalog states 120 and its Freshman/2nd-semester total (14) disagrees with the listed courses (15). | Documented in `CE.json` `known_discrepancies`; validator warns instead of failing. | Ask the CAEE advisor which row is stale. |
| 4 | CS curriculum comes from the **handbook** (Fall 2023 plan), not the catalog page. Other CS tracks (pre-calculus, cyber, health informatics, CC transfer) not encoded. | Main CS track only. | Save the catalog CS page and diff; add tracks if needed. |
| 5 | Gen Ed approved lists (Humanities, Social/Behavioral, African American, Global) come from the **CS handbook p.17** and are applied to all majors. | Other colleges' lists may differ slightly. | Confirm with advisors. |
| 6 | Technical/science electives for ISE, CE, and part of ME have **no machine-readable list** in the catalog guide (ISE footnote: "See your academic advisor"). They use `fill: "attest"` (profile must attest) or `fill: "partial"`. | The audit cannot auto-credit those slots. | Get the approved lists from the departments. |
| 7 | 73 courses have **inferred credits** (`credits_inferred: true`, mostly elective-list courses with no description in our sources) and 63 have **no title** (`title_missing`). | Credit counts for those electives may be off. | Fill from the catalog. |
| 8 | Not enforced: minimum grades (C / C-), non-course prerequisites (standing, permission, "as ISE major", placement scores, "or equivalent"), "max 2 per area" (EE), science Group 1/2 rule (CS). Stored as data/notes. | Audit may over-credit in rare cases. | Out of scope for the checkpoint; documented. |
| 9 | 3 title conflicts between sources (GEEN 111, COMP 160, COMP 285): catalog title kept. | Cosmetic. | none |
